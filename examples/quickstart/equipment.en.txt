This is a fictional equipment-loan exercise for Lab L only. Cross-lab transfers and automatic purchases are outside scope.

A lab is identified by its lab ID. Each equipment item has one equipment ID and belongs to exactly one lab.

Equipment is recorded in the equipment table: id identifies the item and lab_id identifies its lab. The state field is 'in_store' for items in storage; disabled is 0 for enabled items and 1 for disabled items.

Only items in storage that are not disabled are eligible to borrow. A rule definition does not authorize an Agent to perform a loan.

Available equipment count is the number of distinct equipment IDs in Lab L with state='in_store' and disabled=0, at the requested point in time. The unit is items. Other labs, items not in storage and disabled items are excluded; duplicate records of the same ID count once.

No inventory observations, borrower permissions or loan execution connector are supplied. The definition alone cannot answer how many items are available now or execute a loan.
