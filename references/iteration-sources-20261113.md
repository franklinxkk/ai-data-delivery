Next discussion: a read-only metadata adapter could submit source revisions into the same impact/proposal path. Automatically applying changes should require a bounded, previously agreed equivalence rule and affected-question regression. Renaming a reliably identified physical field can be different from changing customer identity, deduplication grain, currency, tenancy or business responsibility. The latter need semantic evidence. No new connector, background monitor or automatic publication is included in 0.3.1. / 下一步可讨论只读元数据连接器，把来源修订接入同一影响／候选链路。自动应用须有事先约定、可判定的等价规则，并通过受影响问题回归。能够可靠识别的物理字段改名，与客户身份、去重粒度、币种、租户或业务责任变化应区别处理，后者需要业务依据。0.3.1 未新增连接器、后台监控或自动发布。
